## 0.2 Plans and billing — one five-plan catalog (decided 14 Nov 2026)

The add-on model of 2 Nov 2026 is **retired**. The product follows the V2 spec: **one catalog for the whole product** (CV builder, cover letters, job tracker and career tools), owned by V1's Stripe account, customer, webhook and event ledger. Source of truth for prices, allowances and packs: `packages/career-shared/src/catalog.ts` (catalog 2026-10-10).

| | Free | Starter | Professional (recommended) | Career Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly / Annual (USD) | $0 | $21.99 / $219.90 | $38.99 / $389.90 | $68.99 / $689.90 | $208.00 / $2,080.00 |
| CVs / cover letters (capacity) | 1 / 1 | 5 / 5 | 20 / 20 | 50 / 50 | 20 / 20 per owner |
| AI document credits / month | 3 | 20 | 60 | 150 | 500 pooled |
| Résumé analyses / month | 3 | 15 | 60 | 150 | 500 pooled |
| PDF export | unlimited, small "Made with FlacronCV" mark | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited | unlimited |
| DOCX | — | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| Tracked applications | 25 | 100 | 500 | 2,000 | 500 per candidate |

Career-tool allowances (action packs, interviews, voice, skill reports, alerts, evidence, …) are the catalog's. Annual is paid upfront; allowances renew monthly on the billing anniversary (Free: account anniversary). Professional has a card-required 7-day trial for first-time subscribers (`PLAN_TRIAL_DAYS`, a code constant). Business always offers Contact Sales.

**Legacy plans** (Pro, Career Accelerator, Enterprise) keep their limits, prices, Stripe price ids and store products, so existing customers renew unchanged. They are never offered to new customers and `POST /payments/create-checkout-session` refuses them (`PLAN_NOT_AVAILABLE`), **except** for the mobile app build already in the stores, which only knows Pro/Enterprise: a native mobile request (`X-Device-Token` and no browser `Origin`) may still buy legacy Pro/Enterprise at the legacy price (Pro keeps its old first-time trial) until the updated app ships (`apps/api/src/modules/payment/legacy-mobile-checkout.ts`; remove it then). The web cannot take that path.

**Selling.** Plan prices come only from V1 API env `STRIPE_PRICE_<PLAN>_<MONTHLY|ANNUAL>`; unset means `503 PLAN_NOT_CONFIGURED` and "Coming soon". Packs and capacity add-ons: `STRIPE_PRICE_PACK_<ID>` (one-time) / `STRIPE_PRICE_PACK_<ID>_RECURRING`; unset means not on sale. Webhooks grant the plan from the **price id** (env map), never client metadata (metadata only for legacy sessions). Subscribers change plan with `POST /payments/change-plan/preview` + `/payments/change-plan`: an **upgrade applies now** (Stripe prorates and invoices the difference; V1 limits rise and the career allowance adds the difference), a **downgrade applies at the end of the paid period** (Stripe subscription schedule, no proration; V1 and career keep the paid allowance until the renewal, then switch together). Store-billed accounts use the store. The Stripe portal configuration should not allow mid-period downgrades either (client setting).

**Stripe test catalog (prepared 16 Nov 2026, not applied).** `apps/api/scripts/stripe-provision-catalog.ts` (core: `src/modules/payment/stripe-catalog-provision.ts`) creates the 4 paid plans × 2 prices and the 24 Stripe-sold add-ons in **test mode only**, tagged `app_id=flacron-cv`, lookup keys `fcv_<plan>_<monthly|annual>` / `fcv_pack_<id>`, dry run unless `--apply`, `--account=acct_…` required. It never adopts, edits or archives anything and stops on any conflict. Target test account confirmed by the operator: `acct_1GnweYIj9WPIsU7I`. **Open:** the Stripe account the live API uses today has NOT been verified; verify it authoritatively before any production key change, price migration or deployment. **Applied in test mode 16 Nov 2026:** 28 products + 32 prices created and read back; a second dry run reuses all of them; the account's other objects were unchanged. The test mapping lives only in the git-ignored `apps/api/.env.stripe-test-catalog.local` (not loaded by the API).

**Billing defects to fix next (not changed yet):** (1) the Professional trial is marked used (`hasUsedTrial`) when the Checkout session is *created*, so abandoning checkout forfeits the trial; (2) ~~pack/add-on checkout did not check that the feature behind the pack is live~~ — fixed locally 16 Nov 2026: `apps/api/src/modules/payment/purchase-availability.ts` refuses a pack (`503 ADDON_UNAVAILABLE`, reason `not_built` / `feature_off` / `provider_missing` / `service_unavailable`) unless its feature is built, its operator switch is on and its providers are ready, as reported by the career service's `GET /access` (`providers`, booleans only) for the caller's own token; fails closed without `CAREER_API_URL`. The web shows the same reason (`career-billing/status` → `available`, `unavailableReason`).

**Stripe key for local tests:** the local API `.env` files hold an unrestricted `sk_test_` key, not FlacronCV's own `rk_test_`; the worktree `.env` also has `APP_ID=flacroncv-local`. **One metering ledger.** With `CAREER_API_URL` set on the V1 API, V1 AI document credits and résumé analyses are reserved/committed/released in the career ledger (the user's own ID token is forwarded), so a Professional user has 60 document credits in total. Without it V1 uses its Firestore counters with the same limits.

**Career access.** The default `CAREER_PLAN_MAP` is 1:1 for the five plans, and legacy pro→starter, career_accelerator→professional, enterprise→career_pro; the env still overrides per plan (e.g. `free:none`).

**Business pooled usage.** An accepted, active Business candidate whose own plan is Free spends from the Business account (its plan and its pool; ledger rows carry the candidate id). Their records stay in their own workspace; billing routes always show their own account. A candidate without own career access (map says `none`) can still accept the invitation and then works on the Business pool. Leaving, archiving, a cancelled Business plan or deletion ends it at once.

**Provider-dependent (code ready; the UI says "not connected" / "coming soon" until configured):** Stripe prices for the five plans and packs; SES for career alert email (address = the person's verified sign-in email); a speech-transcription vendor; the AI provider key; geocoding (`GEOCODING_PROVIDER` / `GEOCODING_API_KEY`); map tiles; job feeds; push and Telegram adapters; App Store / Play products for the new plans; the assisted-forms browser extension (`NEXT_PUBLIC_CAREER_FORM_EXTENSION`).

**Client decisions still open:** (a) deleting a document frees its slot on the new plans (spec: capacity = active items) vs the 2026-08-19 rule kept for legacy plans; (b) cover-letter capacity = CV capacity (assumption); (c) V1 résumé import still spends 1 AI document credit, while the catalog says import is free with 10 completed imports a day (the career-service import follows the catalog); (d) existing Free users move to 1 CV / 3 credits a month / unlimited marked PDF (existing items stay readable); (e) Business sponsors only candidates on Free (someone paying for their own plan uses it); the alternative is always the Business pool; (f) excess files after the storage grace period are not deleted automatically; (g) the new plans as in-app purchases on iOS/Android.

**Operators.** Career moderation and feature switches require V1's `admin`/`super_admin` claim **and** `ADMIN_AUTH_USER_IDS`.

## 0.5 Mobile and the app stores (career access; written for the retired add-on model)

Re-checked on 3 Nov 2026 against the App Store Review Guidelines page (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/, "Last Updated: October 16, 2026") and Google Play's Payments policy. What applies to FlacronCV, and how far it reaches:
- **App category.** FlacronCV is a multiplatform productivity service that already sells digital subscriptions (Pro/Enterprise) through in-app purchase. It is **not** a "Reader" app (3.1.3(a) covers only magazines, newspapers, books, audio, music and video) and **not** a free stand-alone companion app (3.1.3(f) requires no purchasing inside the app). The applicable rules are 3.1.1 (digital features are unlocked with in-app purchase) and its exception **3.1.3(b) Multiplatform Services**: apps "may allow users to access content, subscriptions, or features they have acquired in your app on other platforms or your web site … provided those items are also available as in-app purchases within the app."
- **Storefront scope.** The 3.1.3(b) parity condition has no storefront exception, so it applies on the **United States storefront too**. What changed for the US storefront (3.1.1(a) and the 3.1.3 introduction) is only that buttons, external links and other calls to action to other purchase methods are allowed there without an entitlement. Elsewhere they need a StoreKit External Purchase entitlement where Apple offers one, and otherwise are not allowed.
- **Consequence.** Unlocking a web-bought Career add-on in the iOS app is allowed only if the Career add-on is also offered as an in-app purchase. It is not yet, so the mobile career tools stay **off by default** (`EXPO_PUBLIC_CAREER_ENABLED`) and should not be switched on in an iOS store build until a Career in-app subscription exists. How App Review treats an add-on in this situation is not documented; this is a reading of the published text, not legal advice.
- **Google Play** (Payments policy, checked 2 Nov 2026): apps that use Play Billing "may not lead users to a payment method other than Google Play's billing system" outside the programmes Google runs for specific regions (US: External Content Links programme with enrolment). There is no Apple-style parity clause. The app shows **status only** on both platforms: no price, purchase button, "buy on the web" text or link (enforced by a test).
- No Stripe Checkout or external payment link was added to the app. Native career purchases are not implemented: they need product IDs, App Store Server API/Notifications and Google Play RTDN wiring for a career product, and a combined rule for Stripe + store entitlements. V1's existing Pro/Enterprise store purchases are untouched and not migrated.

## 1. Architecture and data ownership

| Concern | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer identity (sign-in) | **V1** Firebase Auth (`flacron-cv` in production) | The career service verifies the same ID tokens. It never creates, disables or deletes a sign-in (`RetainedByV1SignInDeleter`). |
| Plans, subscriptions, AI credits, exports quota, Stripe/Apple/Google | **V1** Firestore `users/{uid}` + V1 payment module | Unchanged. The career service only **reads** `users/{uid}` and applies V1's own `resolveEffectivePlan`. |
| CVs, cover letters, templates, Jobs tracker, legal acceptance, support, admin/CRM | **V1** Firestore | Unchanged. Career features read `job_applications` (read-only) for outcome analytics. |
| Career records (facts, evidence + files, drafts, matches, alerts, interviews, voice, action packs, learning, modes, portfolios, scenarios, forms, maintenance, map sessions, business, recruiting, developer keys) | **Career Postgres** (`apps/career-api` + `packages/career-server`) | Former V2 schema, 34 V2 migrations + 2 additive integration migrations. |
| Career allowances (document credits, analyses, …) | **Career Postgres** `billing_accounts`/`usage_balances`/`usage_ledger` | Plan comes from the V1 plan through `CAREER_PLAN_MAP`. Separate from V1 AI credits (client decision D2). |
| Career files | Career object storage (`OBJECT_STORAGE=s3` in production: dedicated private bucket; local disk locally) | Never V1's Firebase bucket (refused at boot). |

**Why a second service and a database.** V2's ~28k lines of domain code depend on multi-row Postgres transactions (atomic reservations, idempotency keys, append-only ledger, foreign keys from applications to document versions, durable cleanup queues). Re-implementing them on Firestore would be a rewrite with new correctness risk. Running the career code as its own process keeps V1's API, its build (`tsc`), its deploy pipeline and its failure domain untouched: if the career service or Postgres is down, V1 works exactly as before (the web shows "career tools unavailable"). Cost: one extra container on the existing AWS footprint plus a managed Postgres (e.g. RDS) — **a new paid dependency that needs client approval** (D6).

**No synchronisation.** No record exists in both stores. Copying a career draft into V1 is a one-way, person-initiated action that creates an ordinary new V1 CV/letter through V1's existing endpoints (V1 limits, tiers and validation apply). V2's former two-way "shared records" endpoints are not served (test: `no-record-sync.e2e-spec.ts`).

### Identity mapping
Firebase uid = V1 `users/{uid}` = career `users.auth_user_id`. A career account is created on the first request **only if** the V1 plan maps to a career plan; otherwise `feature_unavailable` and no rows. Concurrent first requests create one account (advisory lock; tested).

### Entitlement bridge (`packages/career-server/src/integration/v1-plan-bridge.ts`)
`V1 effective plan → CAREER_PLAN_MAP → career plan`. Default map is 1:1 for the five plans plus the legacy mapping in §0.2; `none` turns a plan off. On each request (30 s cache) the career billing account is moved to the mapped plan under its row lock: a change re-grants "new grant − units already used" (never a second month), units the new plan lacks drop to 0, the allowance renews on the anniversary while the mapping holds. `billing_accounts.plan_source/v1_plan/v1_plan_synced_at` record it. V1 balances, credits and subscriptions are never written.

### Old records and new fields
V1 records are not migrated or altered. New career columns are additive with defaults (`plan_source` default `career`). V1 documents without career data simply have none; the career UI shows empty states.

### Exports and deletion
- **Career data export:** `/career/data` → `POST /exports` (works whatever the plan; never creates an account). V1's own JSON export is unchanged; the two are separate downloads (D9: single combined export).
- **Erase career data only:** `/career/data` (fresh sign-in required).
- **Close V1 account (Settings):** with `NEXT_PUBLIC_CAREER_ENABLED=true`, career data is erased first; a stale sign-in stops the deletion with a "sign in again" message; an unreachable career service lets V1's deletion proceed and the **closure sweep** erases the career data later. Without the flag, V1's deletion is byte-for-byte the old flow.
- **Closure sweep:** each career user is re-checked about daily; career data is erased only on positive evidence that V1 closed the account (`isActive:false`/`deletedAt`). A missing or unreadable V1 record never triggers erasure. Covers deletions made from mobile or by admins.
- Retained records follow V2's rules (accounting ledger rows without the sign-in id); V1's retention (soft-deleted user doc, documents) is unchanged.

### Background jobs, retries, concurrency
Unchanged V2 mechanisms: reservation → commit/release with idempotency keys (replay returns the first job), abandoned-reservation release, pg-boss worker (renewals, pack expiry, alert matching/digests), storage cleanup queue with `SKIP LOCKED`, revision conflicts (409) on drafts. Plan sync runs under the account row lock.
